How to Order

Three simple steps — no advance payment needed.

01
Choose Design & Size

Pick a hoodie (Design A / B), the Bundle, or any Acid Wash tee — and your size (M | L | XL | XXL). Please check your size carefully before ordering.

02
Fill In & Confirm

Use the order form on this page — choose design & size, give your name, mobile number and full address, then press Confirm Order. No WhatsApp or Messenger needed.

03
Pay on Delivery

We will call you to confirm, then ship via SteadFast from Khulna. Check the product in front of the delivery rider, then pay cash. Delivery charge (courier bill): Khulna ৳60 • Dhaka ৳115 • other districts ৳135 for a single item. Bundle orders and any 2 acid wash tees: delivery is FREE.

Please note: Check the product at the time of delivery in front of the delivery person. After you have checked and received it, returns or exchanges cannot be accepted — so please confirm your size carefully before ordering.
